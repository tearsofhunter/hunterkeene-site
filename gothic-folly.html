The Gothic Folly

Work, 2026
Burning Man 2026, Black Rock CityLead artists: Hunter Keene and Alex NoerpelFiscal sponsor: Five Ton Crane

A monumental open-air scaffold cathedral shaped by light and participation.

A 60-foot cathedral-inspired sculpture built from scaffolding, utilitarian materials and 35,900 programmable LEDs. By day it frames the sky; by night it becomes a beacon of light and music.

Concept

Reconnect people with shared space through monumental architecture.

Use the visual language of Gothic cathedrals to bridge cultures and identities.

Transform individual energy into a collective experience of light, sound, and scale. A gathering space, a performance arena, a place for reflection.
